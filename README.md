# ScrapXpert Hub

Build a premium, modern B2B scrap procurement website for "ScrapXpert India" ("We Buy Scrap. We Offer Competitive Prices. We Deal in Bulk."). Features: dark charcoal/metallic industrial aesthetic with subtle industrial accents; sticky header with quick contact; hero banner with trust badges and immediate CTAs; direct WhatsApp (wa.me/918750612022 with pre-filled messages) and tel links (+918750612022 and +918285409949), including floating mobile call and WhatsApp buttons; rich scrap category grid with curated high-quality imagery across Metal, Electrical, Electronic, Motor & Industrial, and Automobile scrap with individual "Sell This Scrap" & WhatsApp enquiry buttons; dedicated Bulk Scrap purchasing section; 4-step "How It Works" process; interactive "Send Your Scrap Details" enquiry form supporting photo upload previews; Why Choose Us value pillars; About section; complete contact and responsive footer with local SEO keywords.
